Employee Handbook (Sample)

Company Information

Company: ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. Location: Bengaluru, India

Working Hours

-   Office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
-   Employees are expected to complete 40 working hours per week.
-   Flexible work hours are allowed with manager approval.

Leave Policy

-   Employees receive 24 paid leave days per year.
-   Casual Leave: 12 days.
-   Sick Leave: 12 days.
-   Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward.
-   Leave requests should be submitted at least 3 days in advance.

Travel Policy

-   Domestic travel requires manager approval.
-   International travel requires approval from both the Department Head
    and HR.
-   Economy class should be booked for domestic travel.
-   Business class is permitted only for flights longer than 8 hours
    with executive approval.

Password Policy

-   Passwords must contain at least 12 characters.
-   Passwords must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters,
    numbers, and special characters.
-   Passwords should be changed every 90 days.
-   Password sharing is strictly prohibited.

IT Security

-   Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory.
-   Company laptops must use encrypted storage.
-   USB devices are disabled unless approved by IT.

Expense Reimbursement

-   Travel expenses must be submitted within 15 days.
-   Original receipts are mandatory.
-   Finance processes reimbursements within 10 business days.

Remote Work

-   Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager
    approval.
-   A stable internet connection is required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many paid leave days do employees receive? A: Employees receive
24 paid leave days per year.

Q: Who approves international travel? A: The Department Head and HR must
approve international travel.

Q: How often should passwords be changed? A: Every 90 days.

Q: When should expense claims be submitted? A: Within 15 days after
completing the travel.
